Build arithmetic fluency one equation at a time with Number Pick. Solve a problem, find its result on the number board, and drag the tile onto the equation. Correct answers bring up fresh problems immediately, while clear feedback helps you spot and retry mistakes.

Choose the math you want to practice:
- Addition
- Subtraction
- Multiplication
- Division
- Any combination of the four operations
- Complex mode for longer parenthesized expressions with whole-number results

Set the right level of challenge:
- Set minimum and maximum operand values
- Choose how many equations appear at once
- Choose how many result tiles are shown
- Switch between Standard and Complex equations
- Choose System, Dark, or Light appearance

Learn at your own pace:
- Tap an equation for a brief answer reveal without changing your score
- See immediate green or red feedback after every drop
- Retry equations after an incorrect answer
- Keep practicing as solved equations are replaced automatically

Track progress clearly:
- Correct and incorrect counts per operation
- Totals and accuracy percentage at a glance
- Letter-grade feedback for quick progress checks
- Separate tracking for Complex mode
- A confirmation step before resetting scores

Designed for different screens:
- Responsive phone, tablet, and desktop layouts
- Scrollable number board when a smaller screen cannot show every tile
- Adjustable equation and tile counts for quick drills or longer sessions

Scores and game settings are stored locally on your device, so your practice setup is ready when you return. Core gameplay works offline.

Need help? The built-in Help tab explains the controls, modes, scoring, and settings. The Information tab lets you contact support and review submissions associated with the app. Sending a support question requires an internet connection and includes the details entered in the form along with the displayed app and device-layout diagnostics.

Number Pick is suited to students, families, teachers, and anyone who wants focused mental-math practice without a complicated setup.
